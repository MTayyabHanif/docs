  ### 5.1 Technology & Infrastructure Overview

<ResponseField>
  ### 5.2 Architecture

  Our platform is built around two tightly integrated components:

  - **Checkout** – a customizable checkout flow that runs on your own domain. You can configure design, languages, trust elements, and review integration while order data syncs seamlessly from your store.
  - **Payments** – a fully managed payment gateway connected to multiple licensed acquirer partners in the EU and UK. Payments are processed through regulated financial institutions, ensuring compliance and stability.

  These two parts work together as a single flow: your customer completes the order in your branded checkout, while transactions are processed securely and reported back to your store in real time.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 5.3 Shopify Integration

  Most of our merchants use Shopify, so we built a custom integration to make setup as simple as possible.

  - **Custom App** – You install the Checky Pro app directly in your Shopify store. The app connects products, orders, and customer details with Checky Pro Checkout.
  - **Order Synchronization** – When a customer enters the checkout, all relevant data (cart, shipping details, customer info) is transferred in real time to Checky Pro. This ensures that the checkout is prefilled and accurate.
  - **Custom Checkout Page** – Instead of Shopify’s default checkout, the customer is directed to your own Checky Pro checkout page (e.g. checkout.yourstore.com). Here you have full control over branding, languages, and additional features.
  - **Payment & Status Updates** – Once the payment is completed, Checky Pro automatically syncs the status back to Shopify. Orders are updated instantly in your Shopify admin, so fulfillment and inventory remain fully managed within Shopify.
  - **Shopify Restrictions** – While Shopify does not allow third-party providers to replace their checkout within Shopify Payments, Checky Pro operates as an external checkout app. This means you can use Shopify for store and order management, but still run your checkout and payments independently.

  For step-by-step setup, see Getting Started with Checkout.
</ResponseField>
