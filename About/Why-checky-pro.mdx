<ResponseField>
  ### 2.1 Why Checky Pro

  Running an online store today often means working around the limits of platforms and providers: high fees, redirects, and checkout flows you can’t control. Merchants are often forced to compromise: either accept hidden costs or lose flexibility in how they present and process payments.

  Checky Pro was built to remove those limits. We provide a single platform that combines a high-converting checkout with a transparent and flexible payment gateway. By unifying these two components, we give merchants full control over the last step of the customer journey without sacrificing margin, speed, or trust.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 2.2 Checkout

  - **Lower costs** – Eliminate third-party checkout fees (up to 2% on Shopify Payments).
  - **Branded experience** – Run checkout on your own subdomain with your logo, colors, and copy.
  - **Higher conversion** – Multilingual support, trust-building reviews inside checkout, and optimized load speeds reduce abandonment.
  - **Flexibility** – Control every element of the flow: fields, languages, and customer communication.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 2.3 Payments

  - **Global acceptance** – Support for Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and the most popular local APMs in the EU and UK.
  - **Transparent pricing** – Simple IC++ rates with no hidden platform surcharges.
  - **Fast, predictable payouts** – Clear payout schedules in EUR, GBP, or USD.
  - **Multi-currency support** – Accept payments in dozens of currencies and settle in the one that suits your business.
  - **Built-in risk tools** – Real-time chargeback alerts, automated dispute handling, and flexible reserve management.
  - **Growth without limits** – No arbitrary volume caps. We scale with you, supporting campaigns, seasonal peaks, and international expansion without freezing funds.
</ResponseField>
