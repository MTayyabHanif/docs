## 19.1 Overview

<ResponseField>
  To complete your onboarding, you must provide **Processing History** that shows your company’s past sales and transaction performance.\
  This information is essential for our risk and underwriting review it allows our acquiring partners to verify your business activity, transaction volume, and chargeback behavior.

  The more complete your history, the faster your account can be approved.\
  We prefer to receive **at least 6 months** of processing history, but longer periods are always beneficial.

  You may include reports from multiple platforms if they all belong to your company or to the same beneficial owner (for example, multiple Shopify stores, Amazon stores, or [Bol.com](http://Bol.com) accounts).
</ResponseField>

## 19.3 Accepted documents

<ResponseField>
  The following documents are **approved** as valid Processing History:
</ResponseField>

**CHECK Payment provider reports** (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Mollie, Rapyd, Payabl, Adyen)

<ResponseField>
  - Must show:
    - **Total transaction volume / turnover**
    - **Number of transactions**
    - **Chargeback or refund rate**
    - **Your company or merchant name**
  - Preferably exported in **PDF format** (no Excel or CSV)
</ResponseField>

**CHECK E-commerce platform reports** (e.g., Shopify, [Bol.com](http://Bol.com), Amazon)

<ResponseField>
  - Must show:
    - Your **store name or business name**
    - **Sales totals**, **order count**, and **refund or dispute rate**
  - The platform or account must belong to your **company or UBO**
  - Must be **exported as PDF**, not spreadsheets
</ResponseField>

**CHECK Multiple store reports**

<ResponseField>
  - Accepted if all stores belong to the same company or individual
  - Each report must contain full data visibility (sales, orders, chargebacks)

  WARNING _The key is visibility: revenue, transaction count, and chargeback percentage must all be included and traceable to your business._
</ResponseField>

## 19.4 Rejected documents

<ResponseField>
  The following **cannot** be accepted as valid processing history:

  - **Excel or CSV exports** from Shopify or payment providers CHECK
  - **Screenshots** showing partial dashboards or cropped data
  - **Self-created summaries** or manually edited spreadsheets
  - **Reports without total sales or chargeback data**
  - **Unverifiable stores or unrelated businesses**

  WARNING _All processing history must be official, verifiable, and complete partial or manual reports will not be accepted._
</ResponseField>

## 19.5 How to export  "processing report" from Shopify

### **Introduction**

To make it easier for Shopify merchants, we’ve included a quick guide on how to generate a **processing and chargeback report** directly from Shopify Analytics. This report should include all necessary metrics and be exported as a **PDF file**, not an Excel sheet.

### **Step-by-Step Guide**

#### **Step 1: Open Shopify Analytics**

1. Log in to your Shopify Admin.
2. Navigate to **Analytics → Reports** in the left-hand menu.
3. Click **“Create custom report”** in the top-right corner.

📸 _Insert image showing where to click “Create custom report.”_

#### **Step 2: Select Metrics and Report Type**

1. Choose **“Orders”** as the data source.
2. In the metrics panel, click **“+ Add”** and select the following:
   - **Total sales**
   - **Orders**
   - **Average order value**
   - **Chargeback rate**
   - **Chargeback amount**
   - **Refunded payments**
3. Verify that all selected metrics appear correctly in your report view.

📸 _Insert image showing the metrics selection panel._

#### **Step 3: Set the Date Range**

1. In the top-left corner of the report, click on the **date range selector**.
2. Choose **“Last 365 days”** (or select a full year manually).
3. Click **Apply** to load your data.

📸 _Insert image showing how to select the last 365 days._

#### **Step 4: Run and Review**

1. Click **“Run”** to generate your report.
2. **Review** that your data includes:
   - **Total sales**
   - **Order volume**
   - **Refunds and chargebacks**

📸 _Insert image showing the generated chart and table._

---

#### **Step 5: Export as PDF**

1. In the top-right corner, click the **three-dot (⋮)** menu.
2. Select **“Print”**, then choose **“Save as PDF.”**
3. Name the file clearly, for example:\
    Shopify_Report\_[StoreName]\_[Month-Year].pdf

📸 _Insert image showing the export/print dropdown._

### **Submission Tips**

- Export reports covering **at least 6 months**, ideally 12 months.
- Make sure totals include **sales**, **transactions**, and **chargeback rate**.
- **PDF format only** — Excel or CSV exports will not be accepted.
- If you manage multiple Shopify stores, export one PDF per store.

## **19.6 Final notes**

Providing detailed and verifiable processing history allows Checky Pro and our acquiring partners to assess your business performance quickly and accurately.

Before uploading, ensure that:

- The report is **official** and covers at least **6 months** of activity.
- It shows **sales totals**, **transaction count**, and **chargeback rate**.
- The store or merchant name clearly matches your company or onboarding data.
- The report is in **PDF format**, not Excel or screenshots.

Once submitted, our compliance team will review your history to confirm legitimacy and performance consistency.\
A strong and transparent processing history significantly improves your approval speed and strengthens your long-term relationship with **Checky Pro**.
