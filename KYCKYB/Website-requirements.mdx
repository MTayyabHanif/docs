<ResponseField>
### 12.1 Overview

To activate **Checky Pro Payments**, your **website** must meet specific **compliance** and **quality standards**.  
These rules ensure **transparency**, **trust**, and compliance with **EU** and **UK** payment regulations.  
Your store must clearly show that it is a **legitimate business**, run by a **registered company** that customers can **contact** easily.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
### 12.2 Placement of Company Details

Your **company details** must always be included in your **Terms and Conditions (Algemene Voorwaarden)**.  
This is the **official location** for your business information.

If these details appear elsewhere (such as in the **footer**, **contact page**, or **return policy**) and you prefer not to display them, you may **remove them** as long as the information remains **complete and accurate** in the Terms and Conditions.  

If you choose to keep them visible, they must be **correct**, **consistent**, and **up to date** with your **registration documents**.

The following must be listed in the Terms and Conditions:

1. **Registered company name** (exactly as stated on your registration document)  
2. **Business address** (street, postal code, city)  
3. **Company registration number** (e.g. **KVK number** or equivalent)  
4. **VAT number** (if applicable)  
5. **Business email address** (preferably matching your domain, e.g. **info@yourstore.com**)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
### 12.3 Company Identity on the Website

All visible references must show that your website is **managed by your own company**.  
If it states that the site is “**managed by**”, “**operated by**”, or “**owned by**” another company, this must be **replaced** with your **registered company name**.

Example:  
❌ Incorrect: “This website is managed by WebLaunch Agency.”  
✅ Correct: “This website is managed by [Your Company Name].”

This confirms **ownership** and **responsibility** to both customers and payment partners.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
### 12.4 Verification Process

During onboarding, our **risk and compliance team** checks your website for accuracy and completeness.  
If any required information is **missing** or **incorrect**, you will be asked to **update it** before your account is approved.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
### 12.5 Tips for Fast Approval

- Ensure your **Terms and Conditions** include all required details  
- Verify that your **company name** matches your registration documents  
- Remove or update any **third-party management references**  
- Make sure all **links** are active and functional  
- Use a **professional business email** linked to your domain  
- Include **return** and **privacy policies** in your footer
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
### 12.6 Common Reasons for Rejection

- **Missing**, **incorrect**, or **outdated** company information  
- Text showing the site is managed by another entity  
- **Placeholder** or **demo products**  
- **Broken links** or missing **SSL certificate**  
- Website **not yet live**
</ResponseField>
