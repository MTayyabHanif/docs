**Checky Pro** is the all-in-one checkout and payments solution designed for modern e-commerce.

Our mission is simple: to give merchants **full control over their checkout and payment experience**, without the restrictions, hidden costs, or compromises that traditional providers impose.

### Why we exist

For too long, online merchants have had to choose between flexibility and cost-efficiency. Platforms like Shopify or third-party processors often lock merchants into rigid checkout flows and add-on fees that eat into margins. As brands scale especially those selling internationally these limits only get worse.

At Checky Pro, we believe merchants deserve better. We believe you should **own every step of your customer journey**: from the product page, through a branded checkout, to the moment funds arrive in your account. That philosophy guides every decision we make.

### What we built

Our platform consists of two tightly connected components:

- **Checkout** – A fast, customizable checkout experience that lives on your own domain. You decide the look, feel, and languages. You can even display reviews directly in checkout to increase trust. With our free lifetime plan, you can configure and test everything before going live.
- **Payments** – A flexible gateway that supports global cards (Visa, Mastercard), digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), **and the most widely used local APMs in Europe and the UK** such as iDEAL, Bancontact, and Sofort. Merchants benefit from **fast and transparent payout schedules**, clear reporting, and built-in chargeback protection.

### Who we serve

We focus on ambitious merchants who need a payments partner that scales with them:

- **Dropshippers and high-volume sellers** who want to cut unnecessary fees and increase conversion.
- **DTC brands** that care about brand consistency and customer trust.
- **International e-commerce teams** who need local payment options and multi-currency acceptance without juggling multiple providers.

### Where we operate

Today, merchants registered in the **European Union or the United Kingdom** can onboard with Checky Pro and immediately start using Checkout and Payments. These are our home markets, and we’re actively working to expand into other regions.

### Our promise

Checky Pro exists to help merchants **reduce costs, increase conversions, and grow across borders** without the enterprise-level complexity or price tags. With us, you get transparency, speed, and flexibility built into the foundation of your checkout and payments.
