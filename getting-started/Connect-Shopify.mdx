title: "9. Connecting Shopify"

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.1 Overview

  This guide explains how to connect your Shopify store to your Checky Pro account.\
  Once connected, Checky Pro will sync your store data in real time to provide insights, analytics, and reporting.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.2 Requirements

  <Tip>
    Before you begin, make sure you have:
  </Tip>
  <Info>
    1. An active **Checky Pro** account
    2. **Admin access** to your Shopify store
    3. A **stable internet connection**
  </Info>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.3 Log in to Checky Pro

  <Info>
    1. Go to https://app.checkypro.com/login
    2. Log in with your Checky Pro credentials
  </Info>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.4 Enter Your Shopify Store URL

  <Info>
    1. Locate your Shopify store URL , it should look like:

    <Tip>
      `yourstorename.myshopify.com`
    </Tip>
    <Warning>
      Copy only the part **before** `.myshopify.com` (e.g. `yourstorename`)
    </Warning>
    2. Paste it into the input field in Checky Pro
    3. Click **Continue**
  </Info>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.5 Authorize Checky Pro in Shopify

  <Info>
    1. Log in to your **Shopify Admin**
    2. Go to **Settings → Apps and sales channels**
    3. Click **Develop apps**

    ![68c32aae782d582e4ae50cfb_Develop apps.png](/images/68c32aae782d582e4ae50cfb_Developapps.png)
  </Info>
  <Info>
    4. Click **Create an app**
  </Info>
  <Info>
    5. Enter: **Checky Pro** as the app name
    6. Click **Configure Admin API scopes**

    ![68c32d322cc91a81f1ef00c4_Configureapiscopes.png](/images/68c32d322cc91a81f1ef00c4_Configureapiscopes.png)
  </Info>
  <Info>
    7. Select the following permissions:

    ![68c32ddb2cc91a81f1ef2fa7_Filterscopes.png](/images/68c32ddb2cc91a81f1ef2fa7_Filterscopes.png)

    <Note>
      **Customers** → Read & Write

      **Discounts** → Read & Write

      **Draft orders** → Read & Write

      **Orders** → Read & Write

      **Products** → Read & Write

      **Themes** → Read & Write
    </Note>
    8. Click **Save**
    9. Click **Install app**

    ![68c32f705e406403a6dbcabe_installapp.png](/images/68c32f705e406403a6dbcabe_installapp.png)
  </Info>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 9.6 Copy API Credentials

  <Info>
    1. After installing the app, Shopify will generate credentials
    2. Copy the following:

    <Note>
      - **API Access Token**
      - **API Key** 
      - **API Secret Key**
    </Note>
    <img
      src="/images/68c331687985c9ab62f5b554_apikey_secrettoken.png"
      alt="68c331687985c9ab62f5b554_apikey_secrettoken.png"
      style={{ width:"100%" }}
    />

    ![68c3303e851087fc3a78e880_adminapiaccestoken.png](/images/68c3303e851087fc3a78e880_adminapiaccestoken.png)
  </Info>
  <Info>
    3. Go back to CheckyPro and paste these values into the corresponding fields **(Step 4 of the setup)**
    4. Click **Submit & Verify**
  </Info>
  <Check>
    **Congratulations, you are all set!**
  </Check>
</ResponseField>
