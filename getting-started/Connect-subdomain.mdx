<ResponseField>
  ### 10.1 Introduction

  To give your customers a **fully branded checkout experience,** you can connect your own custom subdomain to Checky Pro.\
  This allows your checkout to appear under your brand (for example **checkout.yourstore.com**) instead of a generic third-party URL.

  Connecting your domain builds **trust, improves conversion rates**, and gives your checkout a professional look.\
  You will set this up by adding a CNAME record in the DNS settings of your domain provider (**Shopify, GoDaddy**, **etc.**).

  <Note>
    This guide includes:

    - A general explanation of how the connection works
    - A step-by-step guide for Shopify domains
    - A step-by-step guide for GoDaddy domains
  </Note>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ## 10.2 General setup overview

  Before starting, make sure you have:

  - Access to your domain provider’s DNS settings
  - A Checky Pro account
  - Decided which subdomain to use (for example checkout.yourstore.com)

  <Tip>
    **Tip: Use a simple subdomain**\
    \
    We recommend using a clear, short subdomain like **checkout.yourstore.com**, **pay.yourstore.com**, or **shop.yourstore.com**.\
    \
    Avoid complicated subdomains such as**secure-checkout1**  or **payment-portal**. The simpler the name, the better your customers will recognize it.
  </Tip>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ## 10.3 Connecting a Shopify domain

  If your domain is managed inside Shopify, follow these steps to connect your **Subdomain**.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 1. Open DNS settings

  <Info>
    1. **Log in** to your **Shopify**.
    2. Go to **Settings** → **Domains**.
    3. Find your **Primary active domain** and select it.
    4. Click **Domain settings.**
    5. Click **Edit DNS settings**

    ![DOmaintsettings.png](/images/DOmaintsettings.png)
  </Info>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 2. Create a subdomain (CNAME record)

  <Info>
    1. Click **add custom record.**
    2. Click **CNAME record**.

    ![CNAME RECORD2.png](/images/CNAMERECORD2.png)
  </Info>
  <Info>
    3. Add a new **CNAME record** with the following values

    <Tip>
      | **Field:**     | **Value:**                |
      | -------------- | ------------------------- |
      | **Name:**      | checkout                  |
      | **Points to:** | in.saascustomdomains.com. |
    </Tip>
    4. Fill in the **fields** and click **Confirm**.

    ![CNAME RECORD 1.png](/images/CNAMERECORD1.png)
  </Info>
  <Info>
    ![CNAME RECORD FINISHED.png](/images/CNAMERECORDFINISHED.png)
  </Info>
  <Warning>
    **Common mistake to avoid:**

    \
    **Do not** point your **CNAME** to your main website domain (for example **yourstore.com**).\
    It must point to **in.saascustomdomains.com**, otherwise your checkout **will not work.**
  </Warning>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.4 Connecting a GoDaddy domain

  If your domain is hosted on GoDaddy, follow the steps below.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.4.1 Open DNS management

  - Go to https://www.godaddy.com/ and log in.
  - From the top menu, click your name and choose My products.
  - Under Domains, select your active domain and click DNS.

  ![GoDaddy DNS dashboard](IMAGE_URL_GODADDY_DNS)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.4.2 Add a CNAME record

  <Info>
    - In the DNS records section, click Add.
    - Choose CNAME as the record type.
    - Fill in the following fields:
  </Info>
  <Tip>
    | Field     | Value                  |
    | --------- | ---------------------- |
    | Type      | CNAME                  |
    | Name      | checkout               |
    | Points to | checkout.checkypro.com |
    | TTL       | Default or automatic   |
  </Tip>
  ![GoDaddy CNAME record creation](IMAGE_URL_GODADDY_CNAME)

  **Tip:** Keep DNS clean\
  If you already have an old “checkout” record in your DNS, remove or replace it before adding the new CNAME.\
  Duplicate records can cause conflicts and prevent verification.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.4.3 Save and verify

  - Click Save to confirm your changes.
  - Wait for DNS propagation — this usually happens within minutes.
  - Go to your Checky Pro dashboard → Settings → Checkout domain.
  - Enter your subdomain (for example checkout.yourstore.com).
  - Click Verify connection.

  ![Successful verification](IMAGE_URL_VERIFIED)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.5 Troubleshooting (if verification fails)

  If your subdomain doesn’t verify after a few hours, check the following:

  - Confirm your CNAME record points exactly to checkout.checkypro.com.
  - Ensure there are no duplicate DNS records for the same subdomain.
  - Wait a little longer — some DNS providers take up to 24 hours to update globally.
  - Try clearing your local DNS cache or testing your domain using dnschecker.org.

  If you still have issues, contact Checky Pro support with a screenshot of your DNS record for assistance.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField>
  ### 10.6 Final notes

  Connecting a custom subdomain allows your checkout to appear fully branded, giving customers a smooth and trustworthy payment experience.\
  Make sure your DNS record is correctly formatted, saved, and verified inside your Checky Pro dashboard.\
  Once connected, your checkout will automatically be live under your own domain.
</ResponseField>
